§1What the seminar covers

Linear RNNs are recurrent networks whose state update is a linear function of the previous state and the current input: something of the form h t = A h t − 1 + B x t . Removing the nonlinearity from the recurrence buys a lot: the hidden state can be computed in parallel across time (through associative scans or FFT-like reductions), so training doesn't sequentially bottleneck on the recurrence.

This is the algebraic backbone behind the recent efficient-sequence-model line: linear attention, State-Space Models (S4, S5, Mamba), and the various gated linear architectures. The seminar likely covers the recurrence formulation, why it can be parallelised, how it relates to attention, and which variants have caught on in practice.
