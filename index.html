A practical companion for Advanced NLP. The original slides and past papers stay intact; the notes underneath unpack the parts that usually need a second pass.

6 lectures 240 slides 2 tutorials 3 PYQs public · MIT

§1What this is

Advanced NLP covers a lot of ground quickly: linear algebra, tokenizers, transformer internals, and efficient attention, with past papers in the mix. It is easy to lose the thread when every new idea arrives wrapped in a dense slide.

Think of this as the set of notes you would want beside the deck. Each slide is followed by an explanation of what it is saying, how the pieces connect, and what is worth remembering. The aim is understanding, not a prettier version of the same bullet points.

→ how to read this

Start with any lecture and read straight through, or use the section links to jump to a topic. Each Polaroid holds one slide; click it for a larger view. The notes point out the key idea, likely confusion, and any math worth slowing down for.

§3How the pages read

Every lecture and tutorial begins with a quick map of the deck, then moves through the material slide by slide.

the shape of each slide entry

The slide. The original image, available at full size.

The notes. A clear explanation of the point being made and the context around it.

Extra help. Where it earns its place, you will find a worked formula, a common pitfall, or a link back to earlier material.

Related slides sometimes appear together when separating them would make the explanation harder to follow. Otherwise, it is one slide and the notes it needs.

✦ callout legend

Aha moments. The central insight to keep in mind.

⚠ common trap

A likely misunderstanding, called out before it becomes a habit.

⚙ mechanism

The exact mechanism or math, set aside for a closer read when you need it.

→ cross-reference

A link to the page where the prerequisite idea is introduced in more detail.
